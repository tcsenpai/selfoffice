Everything an office suite does. With an AI that can touch the file.

Your files stay yours

Every edit, save and conversion runs on your machine. Nothing is uploaded unless you point a model at it, and then only what you ask it to read.

Real Office files

Native .docx, .xlsx and .pptx. Parts of a file you did not touch are copied byte for byte, so a round trip does not break a colleague's document.

The AI edits, you review

Tracked changes in Docs, live formulas and charts in Sheets, slides drawn on the canvas. Every AI turn is a snapshot you can roll back.

PDF done properly

Edit text inside the page. Convert PDF to Word, Excel or PowerPoint on the device, with system OCR for scans.

Ask across your files

Full-text search over your folders, indexed locally. Star the files that matter and ask in plain language: answers cite the file they came from.

Scriptable

New in this fork

Real OpenDocument support.

Open an .odt, .ods or .odp, edit it, save to the same file. Not an export: only what you changed is rewritten, and the rest of the package stays byte-identical. Comments, tracked changes, charts and animations the editor does not handle survive the save.

If a change cannot be patched safely, the original is backed up first and you are told. If the file changed on disk behind your back, SelfOffice refuses to overwrite it. 

# convert from the terminal
selfoffice convert report.docx --to odt
selfoffice convert budget.xlsx --to ods
selfoffice convert deck.pptx   --to odp
